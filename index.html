WEATHER SMART NOTE

Use forecasts thoughtfully

Weather is one input in farm planning. Before irrigation or field work, compare the forecast with actual soil moisture, crop stage and local advice.

Weather provider: Open-Meteo. This panel displays current public weather data when the service is reachable. It is not a farm-specific forecast or agricultural recommendation.
